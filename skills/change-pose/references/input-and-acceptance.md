# Input and acceptance

## Preflight

- Require one clear primary person; ask the user to isolate a subject when multiple people make the target ambiguous.
- Prefer a full-body source for full-body pose changes. A cropped source cannot exactly preserve unseen trousers, footwear, limbs, or accessories.
- Flag severe blur, hidden clothing construction, cropped joints, extreme foreshortening, or heavy self-occlusion before spending a run.
- Keep hands and arms away from the selling garment unless the requested pose requires contact.
- Treat newly exposed garment surfaces and body regions as plausible reconstruction, not source-verifiable fact.

## Acceptance gate

1. The requested pose is visibly and anatomically achieved, including weight, torso, limbs, and gaze that were specified.
2. The same person remains recognizable; face, hair, apparent age, and body proportions do not materially drift.
3. The complete outfit preserves garment type, silhouette, construction, length, color blocking, pattern, closures, pockets, labels, logos, accessories, and footwear visible in the source.
4. New folds and tension are physically consistent with the target pose and material.
5. Hands, limbs, joints, overlap, balance, contact, and perspective are credible; no fused, missing, or duplicated anatomy appears.
6. Background, lighting, shadows, camera, crop, and output count remain within the declared locks.

Fail the result when a commercially important garment detail changes even if the pose itself looks attractive.
