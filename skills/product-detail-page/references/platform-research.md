# Platform research

Research the named destination at execution time because marketplace rules, account tiers, and module inventories change.

## Source order

1. Official seller/help documentation for the exact country and surface.
2. Official API or developer documentation.
3. The user's current seller-dashboard screenshots or exported specifications.
4. Reputable current practitioner material only for design patterns, never as the authority for hard limits.

Record direct URLs and retrieval date. Separate verified rules from recommendations and seller-dashboard-only values.

## Questions to resolve

- Is the deliverable uploaded as fixed modules, independent media, one long image, or a mixture?
- Does the platform render text in native fields, embedded in images, or both?
- What changes on mobile: stacking order, crop, text placement, safe area, or image substitution?
- What are the current dimensions, aspect ratios, formats, file-size limits, module/slot counts, and video support?
- Which content needs alt text, localization, accessibility metadata, or synthetic-media disclosure?
- Which claims, prices, contacts, guarantees, reviews, comparisons, badges, or external links are prohibited?
- Does the account have the required brand registration, theme support, or premium content tier?

## Known patterns to verify, not hard-code

- Amazon A+ uses preformatted modules added one at a time. Official guidance emphasizes product use, feature evidence, professional imagery, native text fields, alt text, comparison modules, and desktop/mobile preview.
- Shopify product pages are theme-driven and accept independent media. Official documentation supports images, video, and 3D subject to theme support; image sizing and rendering are responsive.
- Many marketplace long-detail pages are operationally built from stacked sections. Preserve the independent section files even when the upload or review deliverable is a single long image.
