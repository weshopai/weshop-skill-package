# Acceptance

Accept only when every applicable check passes:

1. Exactly one finished image and one person; no grid, board, collage, annotations, callouts, or disassembled garment pieces.
2. Face, hair, identity, body proportions, pose, hands, gaze, and expression remain recognizable and plausible.
3. Background, lighting, camera, crop, and composition remain unchanged unless explicitly released.
4. Every requested clothing slot changes, and every protected garment or accessory remains unchanged.
5. The outfit matches the bound silhouette, piece list, palette, material family, coverage, and formality.
6. Layering order, necklines, sleeves, hems, waistlines, closures, seams, pockets, footwear, and accessories connect plausibly.
7. Fabric follows body geometry with credible folds, tension, gravity, contact, and occlusion.
8. No extra limbs, fused hands, missing fingers, broken shoes, floating accessories, duplicate clothing, or impossible intersections.
9. No invented logo, label, readable filler text, or unrequested branding.
10. Output count and named direction are correct.

State the exact failed property before one targeted retry. Do not accept a visually attractive image that changes the person or scene without permission.
