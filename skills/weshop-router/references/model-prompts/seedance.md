# Seedance prompt guide

- `seedance-2-5` is best for long single-segment generation, audio-visual synchronization, many multimodal references, continuation, artistic motion, and talking/performance shots. `seedance` 2.0 is the routine image-led cinematic route.
- Write the requested shot as subject, concrete action, camera/framing, setting, lighting/style, and then audio. Use timed beats only when the selected route can maintain one coherent segment.
- Name every image/video/audio by ordinal and role. Separate identity/content references from motion, camera, style, ambience, or rhythm references.
- For dialogue, bind one named speaker and exact line per atomic beat; describe non-speaker reactions separately. Treat generated text as unreliable and overlay exact copy later.
- Current WeShop `seedance-2-5` accepts up to 30 images, 10 videos, 10 audios, 4–30 seconds, six ratios, and required native-audio choice. Current `seedance` accepts 1–9 images and exposes its listed model versions, durations, ratios, and supported audio choice.
- Do not copy vendor-only prompt flags, price claims, or automatic fallback ladders. A known terminal failure requires a reason-specific revision and new linked operation key.
