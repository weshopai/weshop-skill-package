# Channel and portrait research

Before generation, browse the current official channel specification and at least two credible current portrait examples or guides for the subject's profession and market. Record access date and distinguish hard platform constraints from visual conventions.

Research:

- output ratio, minimum resolution, file type, circular-crop safe area and compression;
- seated, standing, or crop-only convention for the intended use;
- torso angle, spine, shoulders, chin, gaze, expression and hand visibility;
- face occupancy and headroom;
- seamless, environmental, office, brand-color, or transparent background expectations;
- background luminance and separation from hair/clothing;
- key/fill/separation light, catchlights, face-shadow tolerance and skin-tone accuracy;
- profession-appropriate wardrobe, jewelry and grooming;
- market and brand tone without stereotyping protected traits.

If the user names no channel, default to a seated corporate portrait: upright relaxed spine, torso 10–20 degrees from camera, face returned to lens, shoulders down and open, hands below the face, mid-torso crop, simple seamless neutral background, soft key near camera, gentle fill, and subtle separation light.

LinkedIn's published profile guidance used in this baseline recommends a current recognizable image, shoulder-to-head crop with the face around 60% of frame, workplace-appropriate clothing, and a simple nondistracting background:
